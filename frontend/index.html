Early Childhood Learning & Safety Hub

Built for Seamless School Collaboration

Dedicated tools designed specifically for each stakeholder in early childhood education.

School Administrators

Manage admissions, approve parent accounts, oversee student records, track tuition fees, audit activity logs, and coordinate school-wide operations.

Teachers & Educators

Record daily class attendance, evaluate student progress milestones, manage authorized pickup verifications, and communicate directly with families.

Parents & Guardians

Track your child's attendance and academic progress, stay informed through the events calendar, communicate with teachers, and register authorized pickup persons.

Essential Features for Preschool Monitoring

Everything needed to manage a secure, transparent, and nurturing learning environment.

Academic Progress

Track developmental milestones, classroom activities, and structured teacher assessments.

Daily Attendance

Real-time roster tracking with instant historical records for administrators and parents.

Direct Messaging

Secure, direct parent-teacher communications for timely updates and questions.

Fee Management

Transparent tracking of tuition statements, partial or full payments, and due dates.

Events Calendar

Interactive monthly calendar highlighting school activities, parent meetings, and holidays.

Pickup Authorization

Verify and manage authorized pickup guardians with complete identification details.
